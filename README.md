# Asya Clay Co. — E-Commerce Business Analysis & Digital Commerce Project

## Project Overview

Asya Clay Co. is a real-world e-commerce project involving the design, configuration and improvement of an online store for a small business.

The project involved translating business needs into website functionality using WordPress and WooCommerce, with a focus on product presentation, customer usability, ordering, communication and the overall online shopping experience.

This case study documents the project from a Business Analysis perspective, including the business problem, stakeholders, requirements, customer journey, business processes, user stories, testing and opportunities for future improvement.

## Business Context

Asya Clay Co. needed an online presence that would allow customers to:

* Discover the business and its products
* Browse available products
* View product information
* Add products to a shopping cart
* Submit orders through the website
* Receive confirmation of their orders
* Obtain the information required to complete payment
* Communicate with the business regarding proof of payment and order fulfilment

The solution was implemented using WordPress and WooCommerce.

## Payment Process

Asya Clay Co. does not currently process online payments directly through the website.

Customers complete the website checkout process and receive an order confirmation email containing the banking details required to make payment.

The customer then makes a bank deposit or EFT and sends proof of payment (POP) to Asya Clay Co. through WhatsApp or email.

This manual payment process reflects a current business constraint and is therefore treated as part of the existing business process rather than as a missing website feature.

## Project Objectives

The project aimed to:

1. Establish a professional online presence for Asya Clay Co.
2. Provide customers with an accessible product catalogue.
3. Enable customers to submit orders online.
4. Improve the customer ordering experience.
5. Provide clear communication after an order is placed.
6. Support the business's existing payment and order-fulfilment process.
7. Translate business requirements into practical website functionality.
